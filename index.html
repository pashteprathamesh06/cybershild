Awareness

Scams evolve. So should your instincts.

Most successful scams don't rely on hacking — they rely on psychology. Recognizing the pattern is often the only defense you need.

⏱ Urgency

"Act within 24 hours or your account will be suspended." Manufactured deadlines short-circuit careful thinking.

😨 Fear

Threats of legal action, fines, or account loss are designed to trigger panic-driven decisions.

🏛 Authority

Impersonating banks, police, or executives borrows trust that was never earned.

❤️ Emotional manipulation

Romance and friendship scams build trust over weeks before ever asking for money.
